How to use it

On a phone: tap “Use phone compass”, hold the phone flat, and turn until the gold arrow points straight up and the Kaaba lines up.

On a computer: face north, then turn clockwise by the number of degrees shown.

Distance to Makkah

…

Along the shortest path over the Earth (great circle).

Tips for accuracy

Keep away from magnets, speakers, and metal tables. If the compass seems off, wave your phone in a figure-8 to recalibrate. Your mosque's prayer lines are the best guide when you're there.
