How it works

Live in three steps.

  1. ApplyFill in the short form below. It takes about three minutes.
  2. We verifyThe MasjidHub team contacts you by email to confirm you represent the mosque, and checks its details.
  3. Your team takes overYou get an email invite to your mosque's dashboard, with a short checklist: iqamah times, Jumu'ah, a description and your first event.

Pricing

Free during early access.

Mosques that join now use MasjidHub at no cost while we're in early access. Any paid plan is agreed with your board in writing first, and nothing is ever charged automatically.

Do we need our own website or tech team?

No. Your volunteers update everything from a simple dashboard on any phone or computer. If you already have a website, link to your MasjidHub page from it.

Who can change our mosque's information?

Only the people your mosque invites. Owners add and remove team members; editors update times, events and announcements.

What happens to our community's information?

People can use MasjidHub without an account. If they make one, only they see their profile, and only your team sees who signed up for your events. Read the privacy policy.

Can we take donations through MasjidHub?

Not yet. Today your page links to your existing donation provider. Giving through your mosque's own provider is planned.

Can we leave?

Yes, at any time. Tell us and we'll take your mosque's page down.
