How it works

Smart, simple, and private.

1. Understands everyday words

“8 to 3” means 8am–3pm. “After dinner” follows Maghrib. “Sleep at 10” means 10pm.

2. Knows each prayer's window

Every prayer can be prayed from its adhan until the next time limit. If you're busy at the adhan, it finds the first free moment before the window closes.

3. Suggests events for you

It looks at your age, interests, and free time, and leaves 20 minutes to get there.

4. Stays on your device

This demo runs in your browser with no API key and no account. Your schedule is never sent anywhere.
