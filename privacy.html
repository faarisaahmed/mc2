Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Privacy policy

MasjidHub has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You can use it without an account. This page explains, in plain words, what we store and who can see it.

Without an account

Your settings (location, prayer method, age group, interests, language, text size), planner drafts and practice chat messages are saved only in your browser. They never reach us. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes them.

To work offline, the app saves the site's own pages, styles and fonts on your device. It does not save your searches or map images.

With an account

If you create an account, we store:

  • your name and email address, to sign you in and email you about your account;
  • your settings, so they follow you to other devices;
  • the events you sign up for or volunteer at.

Only you can see your profile and settings. A mosque's team can see the names of people who signed up for that mosque's events, so they can plan. We never sell or share your information with anyone else.

You can delete your account at any time from the Account page. That removes your profile, settings and event sign-ups straight away.

If you apply to add a mosque

Applications include the mosque's details and your name, role and email. Only the MasjidHub team can read them, to verify the mosque and contact you. They are never shown on the site.

If you help run a mosque

Mosque team members are stored with their name, email and role (owner or editor), so the mosque's owners can manage the team. When someone invites you, we send one email to the address they entered.

Services we use

We keep this list short, and each service only receives what it needs to work:

  • Google Firebase stores accounts, mosque information and applications, and sends our emails.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages deliver the website. Like any web host, they see your device's internet address when you open a page.
  • Google Fonts delivers the site's typefaces.
  • OpenStreetMap and Esri provide mosque locations, address search and map images, only when you use the map, search for a place, or apply to add a mosque.

Children

Anyone can use MasjidHub without an account. Accounts are for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change what we store or who can see it, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

For questions or requests about your information, reply to any email you've received from MasjidHub, or write to us through our feedback form.
