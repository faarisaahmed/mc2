About Us

Faith, community, education, and youth under one roof.

Faith

Five daily prayers, Jummah, Family Friday Night, Taraweeh, guest speakers, and Quran reading circles.

Community

Interfaith activities, community service, Hunger Van, Ramadan Iftar, Free Medical Clinic, and food drives.

Education

Weekend School (KG–9), adult education, Sisters Quran class, post-Isha halaqa, Fajr micro halaqa, and Quran Academy.

Youth

Young Muslims Group, basketball court, game room, youth lectures, cook-off, and youth iftar.

Prayer times: use the Athan+ app for live times (available on Android and Apple). Parking information is on the parking page.
