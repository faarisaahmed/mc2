Why MasjidHub

Most mosques don't have an app. So people miss prayers and miss out.

MasjidHub gives every masjid a beautiful, simple home for its prayer times and events. It also helps each person find what fits their age, school of thought, and schedule, so they can grow closer to Allah and to each other.

👵 Easy for every age

Big buttons, plain words, and one-tap larger text. Built for grandparents and grandkids alike.

🕌 Your school of thought

Hanafi or Shafi'i Asr, and ISNA, MWL, Umm al-Qura, Jafari, and more for Fajr and Isha.

✨ Personal, not generic

Events ranked for you by age and interests, with a planner that fits them around salah.

🔒 Private by design

Your settings stay on your device. No account needed.
