How much zakat do I owe?

Zakat is 2.5% of the wealth you've owned for one lunar year, if it's at or above the nisab.

About the nisab

The nisab is the value of 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver. Many scholars recommend the silver nisab because it is lower, so more zakat reaches the poor. Enter today's price per gram to calculate it. Prices change daily.

This is a guide. For personal situations (retirement accounts, business stock, debts), ask your imam or a qualified scholar.
