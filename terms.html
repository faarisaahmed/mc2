Terms of use

By using MasjidHub you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

Prayer times and dates

Prayer times, Qibla directions and Islamic dates are calculated carefully and checked against trusted sources, but calculations can differ from your mosque's timetable, and moon sightings can move dates. When it matters, follow your mosque.

Your account

  • Accounts are for people 13 and older. Keep your sign-in details to yourself.
  • Use your real name when you sign up for events, so mosque teams can plan.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the Account page.

Mosque teams

  • Only apply for or manage a mosque you are authorized to represent.
  • Keep your mosque's times, events and announcements accurate, and post only content your mosque stands behind.
  • Your mosque owns what its team publishes. You give MasjidHub permission to show it on the site and in the app.
  • We may hide a mosque's page if its information is misleading or its team can't be reached, and we will try to contact the team first.

Respectful use

Don't use MasjidHub to post anything hateful, false, or harmful, to impersonate a person or mosque, or to try to break or overload the service. We may remove content or close accounts that do.

Early access

MasjidHub is in early access. Some features are previews and say so on the page: the community chat keeps messages only on your device, and the donate page takes no payment. We work to keep the service running, but it is provided as it is, without guarantees, and features may change.

MasjidHub is free for visitors. Mosques currently use it at no cost; any paid plan for a mosque will be agreed in writing first.
